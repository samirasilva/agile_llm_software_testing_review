# LLM-Supported Testing Artifacts in Agile Development: A Scoping Review

## Overview

This repository provides the supplementary material for the paper **"LLM-Supported Testing Artifacts in Agile Development: A Scoping Review"**, submitted to the *IEEE/ACM International Workshop on Automation of Software Test (AST 2027)*, co-located with ICSE 2027.

The study presents a scoping review investigating how Large Language Models (LLMs) support software testing activities in agile development environments.

The review aims to identify the testing artifacts supported by LLMs, examine the methods used to evaluate their quality and usefulness, and synthesize the reported advantages and limitations.

The repository is provided to support transparency, reproducibility, and independent examination of the review process.

## Research Questions

The scoping review addresses three research questions:

**RQ1:** Which agile test artifacts are generated by LLMs to support software testing activities in agile development environments?

**RQ2:** Which techniques or methods are used to evaluate the quality and usefulness of the software testing artifacts generated by these models?

**RQ3:** What are the main advantages and limitations associated with the use of LLMs in agile testing?

## Review Methodology

The study follows a scoping review methodology, with reporting informed by the PRISMA Extension for Scoping Reviews (PRISMA-ScR).

The literature search was conducted on August 21, 2026, across five scientific databases:

- Scopus
- IEEE Xplore
- Web of Science
- ACM Digital Library
- SpringerLink

The selection process involved identification, duplicate removal, title and abstract screening, full-text assessment, and data extraction.

The initial search retrieved **56 records**. After removing 18 duplicates, 38 records were screened based on their titles and abstracts. Of these, 18 studies underwent full-text assessment, resulting in **13 primary studies** included in the final review.

Two researchers independently assessed the full texts and extracted relevant data. Disagreements were resolved through discussion and consensus.

## Scope of the Review

The review investigates the application of LLMs to software testing activities associated with agile development practices.

The analysis focuses on three dimensions:

1. **Testing artifacts:** Identification and classification of software testing artifacts generated or supported by LLMs.

2. **Evaluation approaches:** Identification of techniques, methods, and metrics used to assess the quality and usefulness of these artifacts.

3. **Advantages and limitations:** Synthesis of the benefits, challenges, and limitations reported in the primary studies.
